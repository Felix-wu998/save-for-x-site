Privacy Policy

Save for X performs its narrow saving function without a developer-operated server, analytics tracker, advertising system, or user account. Videos, GIFs and screenshots are all handled on your own device.

Data processedStorage and sharingPermissionsYour control

Data processed

When you browse x.com or twitter.com, the extension examines the public post structure currently displayed by X to determine whether a post contains an eligible public video or GIF, and to place its buttons. It processes public post links and post IDs for this purpose. It does not read direct messages, typed input, or browsing history.

To confirm that a post contains downloadable media and to choose the highest-bitrate version, the extension sends the public post ID to X's public embed endpoint at cdn.syndication.twimg.com. Video files are downloaded directly from video.twimg.com to your browser.

GIF conversion. X serves GIFs as MP4. When you ask for a GIF, the extension reads that MP4 source from X and converts it to a real .gif entirely inside your browser, using an invisible local extension page. Nothing is uploaded for conversion.

Screenshots. When you click a post's screenshot button, the extension calls Chrome's tabs.captureVisibleTab, which returns an image of the visible area of the current tab. The extension immediately crops that image locally to the post you clicked, produces a PNG and writes it to your clipboard. The image is processed entirely on your device, is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is not retained by the extension.

Storage and sharing

The extension developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, browsing history, downloaded videos, X credentials, or public post IDs. The current product has no developer-operated analytics, advertising, account, or video-processing backend.

Public post IDs are sent only to X for the requested media lookup. Video data is not sent to the extension developer.

Permission purposes

  • x.com and twitter.com site access: identify eligible public posts and place the download and screenshot buttons. No logic runs on any other site.
  • activeTab: read the current public X post URL when the extension popup is opened.
  • downloads: save the selected direct MP4, or the locally generated GIF, to Chrome's download directory.
  • offscreen: create an invisible local extension page that decodes the video and encodes the GIF on your device. It shows no interface and uploads nothing.
  • clipboardWrite: write the screenshot you asked for to the clipboard. The extension never reads clipboard contents.
  • cdn.syndication.twimg.com: retrieve X's public media-version list for a public post ID.
  • video.twimg.com: download the video itself, or read the MP4 source for a GIF you asked to convert.

Optional permission: access to all sites

Screenshots rely on Chrome's tabs.captureVisibleTab, which technically requires either broad host access or activeTab. A click on a button inside the page does not count as invoking the extension, so activeTab is never granted that way.

Broad host access is therefore declared as an optional permission. It is not requested at install time, and downloads work fully without it. The first time you want a screenshot, you enable it yourself from the extension popup and Chrome shows its own confirmation. Once granted it is used only when you click a screenshot button, and the extension still runs only on x.com and twitter.com.

Your control

You can disable or remove the extension through Chrome at any time. Removing the extension stops all extension processing. Files you already downloaded remain under your control in your own download directory.

This review website does not collect launch-list emails or payment information. Those workflows will receive an updated privacy disclosure before public launch.
